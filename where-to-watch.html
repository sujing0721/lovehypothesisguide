Where to Watch The Love Hypothesis

Short answer: Amazon Prime Video, worldwide, included with Prime. Here's the longer answer people actually search for — trials, rentals, and the Netflix question.

Streaming: Prime Video only

The Love Hypothesis is an Amazon MGM Studios production, which means it is locked to Prime Video globally. It premiered September 23, 2026 with no theatrical release — Amazon skipped cinemas entirely, the same strategy it used for its recent rom-com slates.

What it costs

If you don't have Prime: in the US a Prime membership runs roughly $15/month (check current local pricing — it varies by country, and annual plans are cheaper per month). Amazon has also been bundling ad-supported tiers in some regions; the movie itself still needs the subscription, not an à-la-carte payment.

Free options worth checking before you pay:

  • Amazon occasionally runs 30-day Prime free trials for new accounts — cancel before day 30 and you've watched it for nothing.
  • Prime Video is bundled with some mobile/streaming plans (carriers in the UK, Germany, and elsewhere have included it).
  • If you only want the trailer and interviews, those are free on YouTube — embedded on our main page.

Watch-it-tonight checklist

  • Runtime: 1 h 52 min — fits a normal evening, no intermission.
  • Download: Prime Video allows offline downloads on mobile for most originals; good for flights.
  • Accessibility: Amazon originals ship same-day with subtitles and audio description in most regions.
  • Region caveat: catalogs vary — if you travel, your home catalog may not follow you abroad.

The one question everyone asks

"Is it on Netflix?" No — and it won't be for the foreseeable future. If a headline or clickbait thumbnail claims otherwise, it's describing a different rom-com (or just farming clicks).
