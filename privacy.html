Privacy Policy

This page explains what happens to your data when you visit LoveHypothesisGuide. We keep this deliberately minimal.

1. Information we collect

We do not require accounts and we do not knowingly collect personal information. If you email us via the contact page, we store your address only to reply, and we do not share it.

2. Cookies & advertising

This site may display advertising served by Google AdSense. Google and its partners use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on your visit to our sites and/or other sites on the Internet.

You may opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings. You can also opt out of third-party vendor cookies at aboutads.info/choices.

Where required (e.g. EU/UK visitors), a consent management banner governs non-essential cookies before they are set.

3. Embedded content

Pages may embed the official YouTube trailer via YouTube's privacy-enhanced (youtube-nocookie) domain, and content from other sites is displayed in frames. Embedded providers may collect analytics data per their own policies.

4. Analytics

We may use privacy-conscious, aggregate analytics (no personal identifiers) to understand which guides need updating.

5. Affiliate links

If and when we link to book retailers, those links may be affiliate links that earn us a commission at no cost to you. This will be disclosed.

6. Children

Our content targets adults and young adults; we do not intend to collect data from children under 13 (or 16 in the EEA/UK).

7. Changes & contact

We update this policy when tools change — see the date above. Questions: use our contact page.
