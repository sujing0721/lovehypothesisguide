The Ali Hazelwood Reading Order That Actually Makes Sense

The movie just converted a million people into readers. Good news: every Hazelwood novel stands alone. Better news: they share one connected universe, so there is an order that rewards you most. Here it is.

The rule in one sentence

Read The Love Hypothesis first, then Problematic Summer Romance, then anything you like — every other book is a standalone in the same universe with cameos you'll enjoy more once you know the couples.

If you only have time for two

  1. The Love Hypothesis — the movie's source, and the side-plot scenes the film couldn't film.
  2. Problematic Summer Romance — the closest thing to a sequel this universe has, and the most likely next adaptation. If Amazon greenlights a follow-up film, this is almost certainly the book it'll be.

Books like it from other authors

If the movie's Olive/Adam dynamic is what hooked you, these are the classic next reads:

  • The True Love Experiment (Christina Lauren) — fake dating on a reality dating show; the closest match for the panic-comedy of Olive's worst idea ever.
  • Happy Place (Emily Henry) — if it's the chaotic friend group and wedding energy you loved.
  • People We Meet on Vacation (Emily Henry) — for the banter-first, slow-burn romance.
