About us

Who we are

LoveHypothesisGuide is a small, independent fan reference site covering the 2026 Prime Video film The Love Hypothesis and Ali Hazelwood's novels. We publish spoiler-conscious guides: where to watch, book-vs-film comparisons, reading order, and adaptation news.

Editorial independence

We are not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to Amazon, MGM, MRC, Ali Hazelwood, or the publishers of the source novels. All film stills, trailers, and embedded media belong to their respective owners and are used for identification and commentary. Plot descriptions are based on the published novel and the film as released September 23, 2026.

How we make money

This site is supported by advertising (Google AdSense) and, in future, affiliate links. Ads are served by third-party networks; affiliate purchases never cost you more. Our editorial opinions are independent of revenue.

Accuracy

Release-week facts move fast. We date-stamp every page and correct errors on request via the contact page.
